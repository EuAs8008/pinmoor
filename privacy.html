PinMoor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026 Developer: Yong Hing Wong ("we", "us") Contact: yhwong.apps@gmail.com

PinMoor is a pin-board app for iPhone and iPad. We built it to work fully offline. Your boards, photos, notes, recordings and settings stay on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-up is needed.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not run servers that receive your data.

2. Data that stays on your device

What Why Where it goes
Photos you pick or take (camera / selfie) Pinned on your boards, used as backgrounds or photo pins Copied into the app's private storage on your device only
Voice recordings and sound files you add Soundtrack for video clips you make Your device only
Boards, notes, text, settings Saving your work Your device only (app storage)
Pro purchase status Remembering your unlock Your device; purchase records are held by Apple

If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data. We cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

3. Permissions

PinMoor only asks for a permission when you use the feature that needs it. You can change these at any time in iOS Settings → PinMoor.

  • Photos: to pin your photos, and to save finished boards and videos to your library.
  • Camera: to take photos and selfies for your boards and photo pins.
  • Microphone: only when you choose "Record voice" for a video clip.

4. Sharing that you start

When you use Share or Send board to a friend, iOS opens its share sheet and you choose where the image, video or .pinmoor file goes (for example Messages, WhatsApp, AirDrop or Mail). That transfer is handled by Apple and the app you choose, under their own privacy policies. PinMoor does not upload anything itself.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases, restores and offer-code redemptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple only tells the app whether your Apple ID owns PinMoor Pro.

6. Children

PinMoor is rated 4+ and has no social features, chat, accounts or ads. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Security

Your data stays inside iOS's app sandbox, which iOS protects together with your device passcode.

8. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete all PinMoor data at any time by deleting the app. If you have questions under laws such as Malaysia's PDPA 2010, the GDPR or the CCPA, email us. We will reply within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version on this page.
