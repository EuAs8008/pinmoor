Frequently asked questions

Where are my boards saved? On your iPhone or iPad only. PinMoor works fully offline and has no account or cloud. Your boards are included in your normal iCloud or computer device backup. To keep a copy of a board, use Download → Save to Photos.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back? Sign in with the same Apple ID, open PinMoor, then go to Settings → Restore purchase. PinMoor also checks automatically whenever the App Store is reachable.

I have an offer code. Go to Settings → Redeem code, or open the App Store app → your profile picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code.

How do I move, resize or rotate things? Drag with one finger to move an item. With an item selected, pinch or twist with two fingers anywhere on the board to resize or rotate it. Drag an item's pin to re-pin it somewhere else; it will hang and swing.

How do I spin a badge? Flick the outer edge of the badge in a circle. Drag the middle of the badge to move it.

Why can't I save a video in the test version? Saving MP4 videos needs the App Store or TestFlight version, and it is a Pro feature. You can watch the preview in every version.

Can I use songs in my video clips? Only use audio you own or have the rights to. You are responsible for the music you add (see the Terms of Use).

How do I send a board to a friend? Go to Download → Send board to a friend…, then choose WhatsApp, AirDrop, Messages and so on. Your friend opens the .pinmoor file with PinMoor, adds their own photos and can send it back. Sending and opening board files is a Pro feature.

How do I see the tutorial again? Go to Settings → Show tutorial.

How do I delete my data? Delete a board by long-pressing it on the home screen. To delete everything, delete the app.
